LibVLC and libvlccore use LGPL v2.1 or later (COPYING.LIB).
The official VLC distribution includes plugins under various licenses; see COPYING.txt and source licensing headers.
CineScape dynamically loads LibVLC; compatible DLLs and plugins may be replaced.
No standalone VLC interface or VLC installation is required.

The included vlc-3.0.21.tar.xz is the upstream source archive:
https://download.videolan.org/pub/videolan/vlc/3.0.21/vlc-3.0.21.tar.xz
Dependency build recipes and source locations are in contrib/src inside that archive.

MKV is decoded on demand with LibVLC, using its audio clock. No whole-film conversion, generated movie or temporary movie cache is used.
Frames are transferred to the cinema screen, fitted inside 1920x1080 with black bars as needed.
This integration uses CPU decoding and memory frame callbacks; it does not promise hardware decoding, HDR tone mapping or high-frame-rate 4K playback.
Subtitles and track switching are currently disabled.
